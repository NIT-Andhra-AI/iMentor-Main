Curriculum Foundations & Mathematical Formulation

Electrical circuit dynamics are governed by Kirchhoff's laws and differential energy-storage dynamics in inductors (\(v = L rac{di}{dt}\)) and capacitors (\(i = C rac{dv}{dt}\)).

$$ L rac{d^2 i(t)}{dt^2} + R rac{di(t)}{dt} + rac{1}{C} i(t) = 0 \implies s^2 + 2lpha s + \omega_0^2 = 0, \quad lpha = rac{R}{2L}, \quad \omega_0 = rac{1}{\sqrt{LC}} $$
