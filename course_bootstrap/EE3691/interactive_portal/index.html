Curriculum Foundations & Mathematical Formulation

Machine Learning models optimize parameter tensors \(\mathbf{w}\) to minimize regularized risk over complex feature manifolds via gradient descent and convex/non-convex loss surfaces.

$$ \min_{\mathbf{w}} \mathcal{L}(\mathbf{w}) = rac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} \ell(f(\mathbf{x}_i; \mathbf{w}), y_i) + rac{\lambda}{2} \|\mathbf{w}\|^2, \quad \mathbf{w}^{(t+1)} = \mathbf{w}^{(t)} - \eta abla \mathcal{L}(\mathbf{w}^{(t)}) + eta (\mathbf{w}^{(t)} - \mathbf{w}^{(t-1)}) $$
