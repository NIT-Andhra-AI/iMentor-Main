Curriculum Foundations & Mathematical Formulation

Power Electronics converters modulate high-frequency semiconductor switches (MOSFETs/IGBTs) with inductive-capacitive filters to achieve high-efficiency DC-DC and DC-AC energy conversion.

$$ V_o = rac{V_{in}}{1 - D} \quad ext{(Boost)}, \quad V_o = D V_{in} \quad ext{(Buck)}, \quad \Delta I_L = rac{V_{in} D}{L f_{sw}}, \quad ext{THD} = rac{\sqrt{\sum_{n=2}^{\infty} V_n^2}}{V_1} $$
