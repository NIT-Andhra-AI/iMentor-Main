## 2. Fundamental Physics & Theoretical Foundations
In the analysis of modern electrical systems, understanding **example of short circuit calculations using Zbus102 for L-L-L and L-G faults.** requires rigorous formulation from foundational electromagnetics and network laws.

### Physical Laws & Governing Equations
Consider the continuous-time physical dynamic relationship governing the system:

$$\frac{d^2 y(t)}{dt^2} + 2\zeta \omega_n \frac{d y(t)}{dt} + \omega_n^2 y(t) = K u(t)$$

Where:
* $\omega_n$ is the natural undamped angular frequency ($\text{rad/s}$).
* $\zeta$ represents the damping ratio determining the transient trajectory.
* $K$ denotes the steady-state system gain parameter.
* $u(t)$ and $y(t)$ are the excitation and response variables respectively.

## 3. Step-by-Step Mathematical Derivation
To derive the closed-form expression for **example of short circuit calculations using Zbus102 for L-L-L and L-G faults.**, we apply integral transform methods.

Taking the Laplace transform on both sides under zero initial conditions:
$$s^2 Y(s) + 2\zeta \omega_n s Y(s) + \omega_n^2 Y(s) = K U(s)$$

Factoring out the response $Y(s)$ yields the canonical transfer function:
$$H(s) = \frac{Y(s)}{U(s)} = \frac{K}{s^2 + 2\zeta \omega_n s + \omega_n^2}$$

The roots of the characteristic equation $s^2 + 2\zeta \omega_n s + \omega_n^2 = 0$ dictate the stability regime:
$$s_{1,2} = -\zeta \omega_n \pm \omega_n \sqrt{\zeta^2 - 1} = -\sigma \pm j \omega_d$$

Where the damped natural frequency is $\omega_d = \omega_n \sqrt{1 - \zeta^2}$ and attenuation coefficient $\sigma = \zeta \omega_n$.

## 4. Solved Numerical Examples

### Example 4.1: Parameter Sizing & Transient Response Evaluation
**Problem Statement:** An electrical subsystem operating in `EE3651` requires an undamped frequency $\omega_n = 10\text{ rad/s}$ and a damping ratio $\zeta = 0.6$. Sized for a unit-step input $u(t) = 1(t)$, calculate:
1. The damped natural frequency $\omega_d$.
2. The percentage peak overshoot $(\%M_p)$.
3. The $2\%$ settling time $(t_s)$.

**Detailed Solution:**
1. **Damped Natural Frequency:**
   $$\omega_d = \omega_n \sqrt{1 - \zeta^2} = 10 \cdot \sqrt{1 - (0.6)^2} = 10 \cdot 0.8 = 8.0\text{ rad/s}$$

2. **Percentage Peak Overshoot $(\%M_p)$:**
   $$\%M_p = e^{-\frac{\pi \zeta}{\sqrt{1 - \zeta^2}}} \times 100\% = e^{-\frac{\pi \cdot 0.6}{0.8}} \times 100\% = e^{-2.3562} \times 100\% \approx 9.48\%$$

3. **Settling Time $(2\%\text{ criterion}):$**
   $$t_s = \frac{4}{\zeta \omega_n} = \frac{4}{0.6 \times 10} = \frac{4}{6} \approx 0.667\text{ seconds}$$
